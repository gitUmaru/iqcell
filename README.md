<p align="center">IQCELL is an analysis tool developed in Zandstra lab to infer, analyzes, and simulates gene regulatory networks (GRNs) from the single-cell scRNA-seq data of the cells undergoing development.</p>

## Introduction

IQCELL is a platform to infer, simulate, and study executable logical GRNs directly from single cell sequencing data.
IQCELL is an integrative platform that includes modules for gene selection, building logical GRNs, and simulating developmental trajectories under normal and perturbed conditions.
IQCELL also provides users with a platform to simulate the developmental trajectories of cells.
We show that dynamic simulations of the inferred GRNs resemble experimentally observed gene expression dynamics and capture the effects of genetic perturbation studies.
IQCELL offers a versatile tool to infer and simulate GRNs in dynamic biological systems.
